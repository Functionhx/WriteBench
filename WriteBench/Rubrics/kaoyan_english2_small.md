# Kaoyan English II — small essay
Version: 2026.09-v1
Scale: 0–10, half-point estimates. Around 100 words. This is an app-owned practice rubric, not official examiner guidance.
Priorities: every bullet the prompt asks for and the communicative purpose; correct email/letter/notice format; tone suited to the recipient; clear order; accurate, natural language. English II small essays are practical and everyday; reward clear, appropriate communication over ornate wording. Evaluate underlength through missing content, not an invented fixed penalty. Follow the prompt's signature and address requirements.
Anchors: 9–10 covers all requirements clearly with appropriate format and very few language errors; 7–8 mostly complete and coherent with occasional errors; 5–6 basic purpose achieved but uneven coverage or frequent errors; 3–4 incomplete purpose and meaning often obstructed; 1–2 very limited relevant communication; 0 blank or wholly unrelated.
A missing requested bullet, wrong recipient or wrong text type is a task-completion issue. Keep diagnostics on 0–10 separately from the overall score.
