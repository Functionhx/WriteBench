# 考研英语一 · 英译汉（划线句翻译）
Version: 2026.10-v1. Practice rubric following the published Kaoyan English I translation rules and the common 踩点给分 marking practice; not an official marking manual.

Task: translate only the five numbered underlined segments (usually (46)–(50)) into Chinese; the rest of the passage is context. Each segment is worth 2 points; the question is worth 10.

Mark each segment by scoring points (踩点给分):
1. Split the source segment into 3–4 meaning groups (意群) at its natural divisions: punctuation, subordinate clauses (that / which / who / when / although …), coordinated clauses (and / but / or …), prepositional, participial and infinitive phrases, and a long subject. Give each group a share of the 2 points (normally 0.5 each; a key main-clause group may carry 1) so the shares sum to exactly 2.
2. Award a group's share when its meaning is rendered accurately and completely; give half the share for a partially correct rendering; give 0 for an omitted, mistranslated or reversed group. Segment scores use 0.5 steps (0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2).
3. Official caps: if the segment's translation clearly departs from the original meaning, the segment scores at most 0.5. If the student offers two or more renderings of the same item and any one is wrong, mark it as wrong.
4. Typos (错别字) are counted cumulatively across the whole question: three or more deduct 0.5 from the total; fewer deduct nothing. Report any typo deduction in minorErrors.
5. Accuracy (信) comes first: main/subordinate structure, referents, logical relations, tense/voice and the contextual sense of words (including familiar words used in unfamiliar senses). Fluency (达) comes second: Chinese word order (attributives before the noun; time, place and manner adverbials before the verb), natural collocation, no translationese that obstructs meaning. Elegance (雅) is not required; do not deduct for plain but accurate Chinese. Accept any correct alternative rendering.

The total score is the sum of the five segment scores minus any typo deduction. Diagnostic fields use 0–10: taskCompletion = accuracy and completeness; language = Chinese expression; coherence = preservation of logical relations; register = appropriate tone. corrections.original must be exact Chinese spans from the student's answer; corrected and improvedVersion must be Chinese. improvedVersion is a numbered reference translation of the five segments only. Report missing segments or groups in majorErrors.
